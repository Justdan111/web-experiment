## What it is

Fort is a tennis club in Abuja — eight floodlit courts, a coaching roster and a
membership table. The club is invented. The booking flow is not: it runs end to
end, in the browser, with nothing behind it.

## How it works

The landing page is one long scroll with six moments built into it. The
wordmark shrinks into the navbar, a ball rolls across a headline, the gallery
zooms image over image, the coach carousel slides, sections blur into focus and
the numbers count up.

Booking opens over the page rather than navigating away from it, and runs four
steps — court, then date, then time, then your details. Picking one moves you
to the next automatically; only the last step needs a button. It ends on a
confirmation with a reference number.

## The hard part

Making availability feel real. If every slot is free it reads as a mock, and if
slots are random the same court shows differently each time you look, which is
worse. So the same court on the same day always gives the same answer, with
evenings deliberately busier than mornings.

The courts also close between two and three in the afternoon, because nobody
plays outdoors through the Abuja heat. Nothing points it out. It is just there
if you look.
